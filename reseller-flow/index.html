Open questions to resolve

raised by the business

Before the automated payment and provisioning stages are wired live, these need a decision — they change who owns the contract and how money is collected.

Contract

Whose contract & T&C is the customer signing?

Spotlight's, ONESYS's, or a co-branded agreement? Determines what the e-signature step in the flow presents to the customer.

Money

Who collects the money?

Does the payment flow through Spotlight or straight to ONESYS? This sets who reconciles and who issues the receipt/invoice.

Gateway

Does ONESYS have a payment gateway?

If yes, the “Pay” stage uses a hosted payment link + API confirmation. If not, Spotlight provides the gateway and pushes status to ONESYS.

Upgrade

How are package upgrades handled?

When a customer outgrows their tier (A→B→C→D by annual document volume), is it a new purchase in Spotlight Secure or an in-dashboard upgrade with a top-up payment?
